ExamShield Secure Online Examination Paper Protection System

🖼️

Designed and developed a secure examination management platform focused on preventing question paper leaks and unauthorized access. The system uses AES-256 encryption, JWT authentication, role-based access control, SHA-256 integrity verification, and device fingerprinting to protect confidential exam papers. It also includes a real-time admin dashboard for monitoring exam activity and security events.
